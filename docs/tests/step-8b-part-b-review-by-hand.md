# Step 8b Part B: your look at the new Library (computer and phone)

> Checked against the screens on 2026-11-13 (new layout).

This is your own look (answer B10), before any ChatGPT check. It changes nothing unless you click "Use this" and then "Draft it with DELA" (you do not need to).
Log in as the test attorney, "DELA Test Attorney", the only login this needs.

## On your computer

1. Go to https://www.lawfecta.co/dela?tab=library and log in as the test attorney if asked. "Library" is pink in the menu on the left, and the bar at the top says "Library".
2. Top of the page: the heading "Library" with the line "Your firm's processes and policies, all in one place." On the right of that heading: the dark red "Create a document" button, the outlined "Import" button and the underlined "Library settings" link. (If the page shows a table headed "Document library" instead, scroll to the bottom of the table and click the underlined "Show as cards" first.)
3. Right under the heading: the wide search box that says "Find a document by name, purpose or ID". Type `conflict`: only "Run a Conflict Check" stays. Clear the box.
4. Under the box: the category buttons ("All categories", "Client intake", "Client onboarding", "Documents and filing", "Billing", "Firm administration") and the status buttons ("All", "Ready to use", "Draft", "Needs your review"). Click a few.
5. The cards, two side by side, grouped under category headings. Each card has the title, one sentence about what it is for, a status, and one button. No IDs such as INT-03, no owners, no versions.
6. On a card with the status "Ready to use (approved)", click the small round "?" next to the status: it says DELA checks how it is written, not whether it is legally correct.
7. Scroll down, click the dark red "Open document" on any card, then click the outlined "← Library" at the top left of the document. You should land back where you were, with the same search and buttons chosen.
8. Below the cards: the underlined links "Recordings", "Recently deleted (2)" and "Show as a table". Click "Show as a table": the table comes back, under the heading "Document library". Below the table, click the underlined "Show as cards" to return.
9. The "Not sure where to start?" box near the bottom of the page, under those links: type `How we send the welcome packet to a new client` in its box and click "✦ Help me create a document". The screen "New document" opens with the card "What would you like to document?". Under it, a box that starts "DELA suggests:" names a structure, says why, and shows the dark red "Use this" and the outlined "Choose another type". Stop here, or go on if you want a test draft.

## On your phone

10. Open the same link, https://www.lawfecta.co/dela?tab=library, on your phone.
11. The menu is a bar at the bottom of the screen ("Home", "Projects", "Ask DELA", "Library", "More"). The cards are in one column, in the same order inside each card. The category and status buttons sit in rows you can swipe sideways.

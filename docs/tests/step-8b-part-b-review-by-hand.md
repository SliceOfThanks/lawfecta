## On your computer

1. Go to https://www.lawfecta.co/dela?tab=library and log in as the test attorney if asked.
2. Top of the page: the heading "Library" with the line "Your firm's processes and policies, all in one place." On the right: the blue "Create a document" button, the "Import" button and the "Library settings" link.
3. Right under the heading: the wide "Find a document" box. Type `conflict`: only "Run a Conflict Check" stays. Clear the box.
4. Under the box: the category buttons ("All categories", "Client intake", "Client onboarding", "Documents and filing", "Billing", "Firm administration") and the status buttons ("All", "Ready to use", "Draft", "Needs your review"). Click a few.
5. The cards, two side by side, grouped under category headings. Each card has the title, one sentence about what it is for, a status, and one button. No IDs such as INT-03, no owners, no versions.
6. On a "Ready to use" card, click the small "?" next to the status: it says DELA checks how it is written, not whether it is legally correct.
7. Scroll down, click "Open document" on any card, then click "← Library" at the top left of the document. You should land back where you were, with the same search and buttons chosen.
8. Bottom of the page: the links "Recordings", "Recently deleted (2)" and "Show as a table". Click "Show as a table": today's table comes back. At the bottom of the table, click "Show as cards" to return.
9. The "Not sure where to start?" box at the very bottom: type `How we send the welcome packet to a new client` and click "✦ Help me create a document". DELA suggests a structure, says why, and shows "Use this" and "Choose another type". Stop here, or go on if you want a test draft.

## On your phone

11. The cards are in one column, in the same order inside each card. The category and status buttons sit in rows you can swipe sideways.

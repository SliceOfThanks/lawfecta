ON EACH PAGE, LOOK AT
- First impression: in 5 seconds, can a busy attorney tell what this page is for and what to do next?
- Clarity: plain words, no jargon, no internal terms; labels that say what a box wants; hints that help.
- Hierarchy and layout: what stands out first, how many cards, how long the page is, whether the main action is easy to find.
- Trust and safety: money, fees, confidentiality, client information and legal responsibility explained where they matter, without fear or clutter.
- Consistency: the same thing named the same way across pages; buttons and cards used the same way.
- Feedback: whether it is clear what happened after an action and what happens next.
- Accessibility you can judge by eye: text size, contrast, crowded lines, tiny buttons, and meaning shown only by color.
- What is missing: anything you expected and did not find.

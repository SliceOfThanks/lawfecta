# The stranger test

Can a person who has never worked with a firm do a handed-over task from DELA's document alone?

This is a test with real people. No automated check can answer it. It takes about 3 hours of your time in total and about 45 minutes from each tester.

## What you need

- **Three testers.** People who do legal support work or could (a paralegal, a legal assistant, a virtual assistant). None of them may have worked with the firm whose process you use. They do not need a Lawfecta account.
- **Three real processes** from a real firm. Yours, or a firm you know well enough to answer questions about it. The three cases below say which kinds to pick. Use made-up names. No client information.
- The switch "Handoff kit" set to "Access list" at https://www.lawfecta.co/dela-admin?tab=switches (group "Documents and files").

## The three cases

Pick processes of these three kinds, because they fail in different ways.

| Case | Kind of process | Example | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | A routine task in one system | Open a new matter in the firm's practice software and save the intake form to the matter | Menu paths, where files live, how files are named |
| B | A task with a handoff back to the attorney | Run a conflict check and report the result; the attorney decides whether to take the matter | Who to ask, what to send back, where the work must stop |
| C | A part of a longer process | Steps 2 to 4 of monthly billing: export unbilled time, fix entries with no description, prepare draft invoices for the attorney | Starting in the middle: what was done before, what comes after, what the person needs first |

For each case, write down in advance, in two or three sentences, what a correct finished result is. You will score against it.

## Part 1: you prepare each case (about 30 minutes each)

Do this three times, once per case, logged in as an attorney.

1. Go to https://www.lawfecta.co/dela?tab=library. Click the dark red button "Create a document" at the top right. Choose "SOP" for cases A and B, and "SOP" or "Workflow" for case C.
2. Answer DELA's questions the way a busy attorney would: short, and from memory. Do not polish. This matters, because the test is whether DELA makes up for what a busy attorney leaves out.
3. When the draft opens, find the card "Can someone new follow this?" in the right column, under "Quality check". Click "✦ Check as someone new".
4. **Write down how many gaps DELA found.** Then, before answering, take a screenshot of the whole document. This is the "before" copy.
5. Type an answer under each question and click "Add my answers to the document". Read the new draft. Click "✦ Check again". Repeat until the tag reads "Ready for someone new", or until DELA's remaining gaps are ones you disagree with. Write down how many rounds it took.
6. Click "Approve".
7. Go to https://www.lawfecta.co/dela?tab=projects. Click "+ New Gig" at the far right of the heading, then "From an approved document". Choose your document.
8. For case C only: in the box "Which steps are you handing over?", untick the steps the firm keeps.
9. Fill in a budget and click "Draft with DELA". On step 2, scroll to the card "The handoff". Check the list under "What the freelancer needs before starting". Add anything missing with "Add something", then "Save the list".
10. Click "Continue to review". Scroll to the card "Exactly what an invited or hired freelancer gets". Take a screenshot of that whole card. **This screenshot is what the tester gets.** Do not post the gig.

## Part 2: each tester does one case (about 45 minutes)

Give each tester one screenshot from step 10 and the tester sheet below. Give them nothing else. Do not explain the firm. Sit with them, or be on a call, and stay silent unless they ask a question.

Say this to the tester:

> "You have been hired to do this task for a law firm you have never worked with. Read it, then walk me through exactly what you would do at each step, out loud, as if you were doing it. Any time you would have to stop and ask the firm something, say so and I will write it down. I will only answer after you tell me what you would do if nobody answered."

Every question they ask is a failure of the document, not of the tester. Write down each one, word for word, with the step number.

If the firm's system is one the tester can open (for example a demo account), let them really do the steps. That is better than a walk-through.

## Scoring

Count for each case:

- **Steps done without asking**, out of the steps handed over.
- **Stoppers**: questions marked "would have stopped the work".
- **The three "before starting" answers**: did each match what you wrote down in advance? (0 to 3)
- **Your own judgment of the result**: would you accept it? Yes, with small changes, or no.

| Result | What it means |
|---|---|
| Every step done without asking, no stoppers, 3 of 3 answers right, result accepted | Ready. This is the bar for the pilot. |
| One or two questions, no stoppers | Close. Send me the questions; the newcomer check should have caught them. |
| Any stopper, or the result is not accepted | Not ready. Send me everything; this is what to fix before inviting anyone. |

Also compare, for each case: how many gaps did DELA find in step 4, and how many questions did the tester still ask? If a tester asks about something DELA never flagged, the newcomer check has a blind spot, and I adjust what it looks for.

## What to send me

For each case:
1. The "before" screenshot from step 4 and the screenshot the tester got.
2. The number of gaps DELA found and the number of rounds.
3. The filled tester sheet (a photo is fine).
4. Your verdict on the result.

I turn every tester question into either a change to what the newcomer check looks for, or a change to how DELA writes documents, and tell you which.

## A quicker first pass, if you want one

Before using people's time, you can get a rough signal in ten minutes: open a new ChatGPT chat, paste the screenshot from step 10, and say "You are a freelancer who has never worked with this firm. Walk through each step and list every question you would have to ask before you could finish." It is not a substitute for a person. A person gets stuck in ways a chat does not, but it catches the obvious gaps cheaply.

WHAT WORKS WELL
1. What happens next gives role-specific guidance on funded monthly work.
2. The scope mismatch warning states the conflicting weekly/monthly values clearly.
3. Library search finds the requested document, and status labels distinguish drafts, review and approved work.
4. Approved document version, owner and review dates make the operational source explicit.
5. Fee disclosures explain both attorney totals and freelancer net payouts; payout setup explains Stripe handles bank information.

- COMING SOON on engagement/payment features that have working pages.
- Complete engagement: milestones set when you both sign / accepted 0 of 0 / paid $0.
- What does what match-ready means mean? (accessibility label).
- Two more welcome packets a week versus 10 to 12 each month.
- Post-approval conditional message does not repeat scheduled terms.
- Message removed without reason.

# Review of steps 74 and 75: code, screens, wording and accessibility

Date: 2026-11-23. Asked for by Stacey: "evaluate issues, risks, etc in the code, UI, UX and accessibility".
What was reviewed: the bookmark tags and notes (step 74), the profile header, and the rebuilt Ask DELA workspace (step 75, all ten pages), as live at code_version 0e69622.
How: two independent reviewers read the code, the styles and the ten mockups, one for the code and one for the screens and accessibility. Claude then checked the main findings against the code. Nothing was looked at in a running browser by a person, so layout findings marked "from the styles" still need eyes on a screen.

Nothing found puts money, permissions or another firm's data at risk. Every action still goes through the server's own checks. The faults are in what the pages show and how they behave.

## E. Things an attorney will stumble on

1. **Two meanings of "instructions".** "DELA instructions" are rules for the AI. "Draft the instructions" on the handoff page makes a new step-by-step document. Side by side, they read as the same thing.
2. **Words from the mockups that are jargon:** "Resolver", "Authorized resolver", "Downstream reviewer", "Accountable owner", "Recipient-safe preview", "Review authority", "Prerequisites & inputs", "Impact review", "Version states", "Working context".
3. **One thing, several names:** playbook, document, SOP; handoff, delegation, selected work; recipient, freelancer.
4. **The gig crosses between Ask DELA and Projects five or six times** and no page says which place is for what.
5. **"Review dependencies & ownership" starts a handoff** without saying so. "Stop this handoff" ends it at once with no question.
6. **"Approve this version" and "Approve the instructions"** act at once. What changes for the firm is said only afterwards.
7. **Seven lines in the second menu**, where the mockups have five, and the menu shows on the chat page only.
8. **The Library does not mention My Playbooks.** Every "Open" leaves the workspace with no way back except the main menu.
9. **The invitation page is reached only from Talent.** The walk from screen 1 to screen 10 still breaks after the recipient preview.
10. **Answers do not show which playbook they used.** Mockup 1 shows this on each answer; it is the point of "playbook-aware".

## F. Where the pages still differ from the mockups

- Screen 1: no source tags on answers; the question is missing from the "A useful next question" card.
- Screen 4: "Next step" is a two-part mini form in the narrow right column, where the mockup has one button. It is the least like its mockup.
- Screen 7: "Before" compares with the current draft, not the approved version. Several main buttons on one page.
- Screen 9: "Recipient questions" is one greyed button, where the mockup has a box to ask a question.
- Screen 10: the event history is real only for team work.

## G. What is sound

- Permissions: tags and notes can be read and written only by their owner, only on a freelancer they bookmarked. Limits come from settings.
- Hidden last names: nothing new exposes a full name.
- The gig draft form sends back every field it does not show, unchanged.
- Approve, request approval, apply and reject use the Library's own commands, with the server's checks.
- Double clicks are safe on every sending or approving button.
- Members without the switch see no change, except the blue profile header and the photo spacing, which were meant for everyone.
- Every page has its own address and puts the keyboard on its heading when it opens. Repeated buttons have distinct names for screen readers.

## H. Two older weaknesses this review ran into

- With the "client details allowed" switch off, the button "It has no client information, save it" skips the sensitive data check entirely. On the site the switch is on, where sensitive data is always stopped. If the switch is ever turned off, an SSN could be saved after one click.
- The form helper used across DELA's older pages does not tie a label to its box. C1 is one case; other old pages likely have the same.
